Black Volga

"The Black Volga" is an old Soviet urban legend about a mysterious car that abducts people and subjects them to horrific fates. You play as a man whose deeds were so heinous that they gave rise to this very legend. Before you died, you managed to repent for your sins; the Almighty showed mercy and granted you a second chance. You find yourself in Purgatory, where the only thing you can do is drive without stopping. The game offers a short but engaging experience; it is merely a prototype, so not all of the protagonist's torments have been implemented yet.
